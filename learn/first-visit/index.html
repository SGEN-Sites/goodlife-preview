Before you go

Good Life Collective has five stores — Rochester, Buffalo, Canandaigua and Batavia in New York, plus Astoria, Oregon — and one rule comes first at all of them: you must be 21 or older with valid government-issued ID to purchase cannabis products.

  1. 01

    Step 1: Grab your ID

    Bring a valid, government-issued ID that shows you're 21 or older. The same rule applies however you order, so keep it handy for pickup too.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Pick your store

    Choose the Good Life nearest you and the site shows that store's menu. Each store stocks its own lineup, so the shelves in Batavia won't match Astoria's. See every store on the locations page.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Order ahead, or just walk in

    Browse and order online for in-store pickup at all five stores. In Rochester and Buffalo you can also get fast, free delivery right to your door. *Free delivery requires a minimum order of $50. Discreet packaging available. Details on the delivery page.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Check the hours

    Rochester is open Mon–Thu 9am–9pm, Fri–Sat 9am–10pm, Sun 10am–9pm. Buffalo is Mon–Thu 9am–9pm, Fri–Sat 9am–11pm, Sun 10am–8pm. Heading to Canandaigua, Batavia or Astoria? Give the store a call before you go.

At the counter

Same promise in Buffalo, Canandaigua, Batavia and Astoria. You don't need the lingo. Tell the team what you've tried before (if anything), what you're curious about, and what you want to spend. Good questions to ask:

  • Which format fits what I'm after: flower, a pre-roll, a vape, an edible or a drink?
  • What sizes does this come in?
  • What's on deal today?
  • Is this one labeled in THC % or in milligrams?

New to cannabis, or new to a format? Start low and go slow. Edibles and drinks can take a while to kick in, so wait before you have more. Everyone's experience is different.

How to read our menu

Good Life's product cards and product pages pack a handful of key details. Here's what they mean.

  • Strain type. Where it's listed: Sativa, Hybrid, Indica, Sativa-Dominant or Indica-Dominant (shortened to "-dom" on product cards). Think of it as a starting point, not a promise.
  • THC and CBD. Flower, pre-rolls, vapes and concentrates show THC as a percentage on the card. Product pages can break it out further, with CBD and figures like THCa, when that data is available. Edibles, drinks and tinctures are measured in milligrams (mg) of THC per package.
  • Size. Grams for flower, pre-rolls, vapes and concentrates; fluid ounces or milliliters for drinks and tinctures.
  • Price. The shelf price, with the price with tax right underneath.
  • Deal tags. On Rochester's menu, look for weekly-deal tags like Doobie Tuesday on product cards.
  • Terpenes and effects. Many product pages list the terpenes measured in that product, aroma compounds like limonene and myrcene. Effects tags, where you see them, are the brand's own.

Filter by brand, size or strain type, and sort by price or THC, to narrow things down. Want the deep dive? Read how to read the Good Life menu and the full product guide.

Deals and rewards

Rochester runs a weekly deal board with a different deal every day: Munchie Monday, Doobie Tuesday, Weed Wednesday, Thirsty Thursday, TGIF (Thank GoodLife Its Friday), Shatter-Day and Sunday Bun(dle) Day. Deals vary by store and terms can change, so check the deals page or your store's menu for current terms. The weekly deals guide has the full board.

Then there's Good Life Rewards: earn points on every purchase and unlock exclusive discounts. Sign up or log in to see your personal rewards. With the Good Life app, you can place orders on mobile, manage your rewards, and never miss out on special offers. On iPhone? Get the app on the App Store (opens in a new tab). On Android, download the app straight from Good Life; it isn't on the Google Play Store.

In Canandaigua, the in-store poster spells it out: spend $1.00, earn 1 point; 100 points = $2.00; apply points to up to 10% of your purchase (pretax total); points never expire. Ask your budtender to join. The app isn't available for the Canandaigua store.

After your visit

  • Consume responsibly. Start low, go slow, and give it time before you have more.
  • Store it safely. Keep cannabis out of reach of children and pets. Our storage guide has the details.
  • Never drive impaired. Plan your ride home before you start.
  • Keep it in the state. Don't take cannabis across state lines. That's federal law, and it includes the trip between our New York stores and Astoria.
Accidental ingestion or overconsumption? Contact the Poison Center at 1-800-222-1222 or call 9-1-1.

Quick answers

What do I need to bring to Good Life?

Valid government-issued ID. You must be 21 or older with valid government-issued ID to purchase cannabis products.

Can I order ahead?

Yes. Browse and order online for in-store pickup at any of our five stores, or for delivery from Rochester and Buffalo. *Free delivery requires a minimum order of $50.

Does every store have the same menu?

No. Each store stocks its own lineup, so pick your store first to see what's on its shelves.

What are your store hours?

Hours vary by location. Rochester is open Mon–Thu 9am–9pm, Fri–Sat 9am–10pm, Sun 10am–9pm. Buffalo is Mon–Thu 9am–9pm, Fri–Sat 9am–11pm, Sun 10am–8pm. For other locations, call the store.
