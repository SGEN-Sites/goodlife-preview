Good Life menus carry flower, pre-rolls, vapes, edibles, concentrates, beverages, tinctures, topicals, CBD and accessories. Not every store stocks every category, and lineups change all the time, so pick your store and the shop buttons below take you straight to what's on its shelves.

Flower

The classic: dried, cured cannabis buds, ready to grind and roll or pack. Every flower card shows the THC %, plus the strain type where it's listed.

How it's sold: in our New York stores, eighths (3.5g) are the go-to, with quarters (7g), half ounces (14g) and full ounces (28g) on the bigger end, plus smaller grabs like 0.7g, 1g and 2g. You'll also find pre-ground flower, moonrocks and infused flower: buds boosted with kief, hash, diamonds or live resin.

Pre-rolls

Flower that's already rolled. The pre-roll shelf goes deep:

  • Singles: mostly 1g, with half-gram and 1.5g to 2g options.
  • Multi-packs: from 2-packs up to 28- and 30-packs of minis, some mixing strains.
  • Infused pre-rolls: flower boosted with kief, hash, diamonds, live resin or live rosin.
  • Blunts: 1g blunts, 1.5g moon rock blunts and 2g infused blunts.

Infused pre-rolls usually carry a higher THC % than plain flower, so check the card and pace yourself. More in pre-rolls: packs, infused and blunts.

Vapes

Cannabis oil, heated instead of burned. Our menu sorts vapes into two types:

  • Cartridge / 510: a cart that screws onto a 510-thread battery, mostly in 1g with some 0.5g. The battery is sold separately: look under accessories, or grab a Good Life-branded 510 battery from the merch shop.
  • Disposable / AIO: all-in-one, with the battery and oil in a single device, mostly 0.5g to 2g.

Pods from brands like STIIIZY and PAX are filed under Disposable / AIO, but they click into their own brand's battery, so check the product name or ask the team. Names tell you what's inside, too: distillate, live resin, live rosin, liquid diamonds or cured resin. Go deeper with 510 carts vs all-in-one vapes.

Edibles

Cannabis you eat. Gummies lead the pack, with chocolates, chews, mints and tablets behind them.

How it's sold: in packs labeled with the milligrams of THC per package. 100mg packs are the most common on our menus, with 10mg, 20mg and 50mg options too. Check how many pieces are inside: a 10-piece, 100mg pack works out to 10mg a piece. Some pair THC with CBD, CBG, CBN, CBC or THCV, and the ratio is right in the name.

Start low, go slow. Edibles can take a while to kick in, so wait before having more, and keep them locked up and away from kids and pets. Read edibles: start low, go slow.

Concentrates

Extracts made for dabbing, or for adding to a bowl or joint. Names you'll see on our menus:

  • Live resin: extracted from fresh-frozen plants.
  • Rosin and live rosin: pressed with heat and pressure, no solvents. Hash rosin is in this family.
  • Textures: badder, budder, sugar, crumble, and diamonds with sauce.
  • Old school: bubble hash, kief and hash.

How it's sold: mostly 1g, with 2g, 2.5g and 3.5g jars and buckets too. Concentrates are strong; the THC % is on every card, and a little goes a long way. Rigs, bangers and dab tools turn up under accessories at several stores.

Liquid cannabis extract in a dropper bottle, usually 30ml (1 fl oz) or 15ml. Most list the total THC in the bottle, from about 100mg up to 1,000mg, so use the dropper to measure small amounts. Some are made to mix into a drink, and some pair THC with CBD or CBN.

Quick answers

What is the difference between a 510 cart and an all-in-one vape?

A 510 cartridge screws onto a separate 510-thread battery. An all-in-one (AIO) or disposable has the battery built in. Our vape filter splits them into Cartridge / 510 and Disposable / AIO.

How are edibles labeled at Good Life?

In milligrams (mg) of THC per package. 100mg packs are the most common on our menus. Start low, go slow, and wait before having more.

Does every Good Life store carry every category?

No. Each store has its own menu, and CBD, for example, has its own category at some stores and not others. Pick your store to see what's on its shelves.
