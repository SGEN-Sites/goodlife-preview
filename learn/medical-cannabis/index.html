Good Life is adult-use

Good Life Collective is a premium recreational cannabis dispensary, and all five of our stores, in Rochester, Buffalo, Canandaigua, Batavia and Astoria, are adult-use shops. You don't need a medical card to shop with us: you must be 21 or older with valid government-issued ID to purchase cannabis products.

To shop, bring valid ID showing you're 21 or older, pick your store, and walk in or order ahead for in-store pickup at any of our five stores, or for delivery from Rochester and Buffalo. Store addresses and phone numbers are on the locations page.

Good Life doesn't describe a medical program of its own, and nothing on this site is medical advice. Have a question about how a medical card works at a specific store? Call that store directly.

New York's medical program

In New York, the medical cannabis program is run by the Office of Cannabis Management (OCM), the same agency that licenses adult-use dispensaries like ours. Who qualifies, how to register and where registered patients can buy are all set by the program, so get the current details straight from cannabis.ny.gov (opens in a new tab).

Oregon's medical program

In Oregon, the Oregon Medical Marijuana Program (OMMP) is run by the Oregon Health Authority (OHA), separate from the OLCC, which regulates adult-use retail. Eligibility and registration details are on the OMMP website (opens in a new tab).

Straight talk on our menus

Effects tags on our menus are the brands' own, strain types are a starting point rather than a promise, and everyone's experience is different. For questions about your health, talk to a healthcare provider.

What we can help with is the shopping part. The product guide breaks down every category we carry, and the first-visit guide covers ID, ordering, deals and rewards. For the legal basics, see New York and Oregon.

Not medical or legal advice. Program rules change. Check OCM in New York or the Oregon Health Authority in Oregon for current details.

Quick answers

Do I need a medical card to shop at Good Life?

No. Good Life's stores are adult-use (recreational) dispensaries. You must be 21 or older with valid government-issued ID to purchase cannabis products.

Who runs New York's medical cannabis program?

The New York Office of Cannabis Management (OCM). Visit cannabis.ny.gov for current program details.

Who runs Oregon's medical marijuana program?

The Oregon Health Authority runs the Oregon Medical Marijuana Program (OMMP).

Is anything on this page medical advice?

No. It's general information. For questions about your health, talk to a healthcare provider.
