Who sets the rules

Oregon's adult-use (recreational) cannabis market is regulated by the Oregon Liquor and Cannabis Commission (OLCC), which licenses retailers like our Astoria shop. For the official answer on anything from possession to where you can consume, oregon.gov/olcc (opens in a new tab) is the source.

Our Oregon home

Astoria is family business for us. In our own words:

You'll find the shop at 193 Marine Dr, Astoria, OR 97103. Call (503) 325-4078, or read our story.

21+ with valid ID

Same rule as our New York stores: you must be 21 or older with valid government-issued ID to purchase cannabis products. And never buy for anyone under 21.

Limits, taxes and home grow

Oregon sets its own rules on how much adults can buy and possess, how cannabis is taxed, and growing at home, so don't assume they match New York's. Those details change, so we don't repeat numbers here. Check OLCC (opens in a new tab) for the current version.

On the Astoria menu

Astoria's menu has its own flavor. The concentrates shelf runs deep, from live resin and live rosin to badder and bubble hash, and the topicals include transdermal patches. You'll also find pre-rolls, vapes, edibles, drinks, tinctures, CBD and accessories, plus Good Life merch like snapbacks, hoodies and tees in the merch shop.

Product cards show the THC, as a percentage or in milligrams. New to a format? Start low and go slow. The product guide breaks down every category.

Where to consume

Where you can use cannabis depends on state law, local rules and the property you're on. When in doubt, keep it private, and check OLCC's guidance first.

Never drive impaired

Never drive impaired. Plan your ride before you start, whether you're heading across town or along the coast.

Keep it in Oregon

Don't take cannabis across state lines. It's against federal law, even when both states have legal adult-use markets. That means no carrying Astoria purchases across the bridge into Washington, no packing them for a trip to New York, and no bringing New York products back the other way. Buy where you'll use it.

Keep it safe

Store cannabis out of reach of children and pets, ideally locked up. In case of accidental ingestion or overconsumption, contact the Poison Center at 1-800-222-1222 or call 9-1-1. Please consume responsibly.

Visit Good Life Astoria

Order online for in-store pickup, or just come by. Delivery isn't offered from Astoria; it's available from our Rochester and Buffalo stores in New York. Join Good Life Rewards to earn points on every purchase, and download our mobile app for perks.

Get directions on the Astoria store page, see Good Life in Oregon, and if you're new, start with the first-visit guide.

Quick answers

How old do you have to be to buy cannabis in Oregon?

21 or older. At Good Life Astoria, you must be 21 or older with valid government-issued ID to purchase cannabis products.

Is Good Life Astoria licensed?

Yes. Good Life Collective Astoria, 193 Marine Dr, Astoria, OR 97103, holds license number 050-1017483E9A8.

Does Good Life deliver in Oregon?

Not from Astoria. You can order online for in-store pickup there; delivery is offered from our Rochester and Buffalo stores in New York.

Can I take cannabis from Oregon to Washington or New York?

No. Taking cannabis across state lines is against federal law, even between states with legal adult-use markets.

Where can I find Oregon's official cannabis rules?

On the Oregon Liquor and Cannabis Commission's website, oregon.gov/olcc. Laws and regulations change, so check there for the current version.
