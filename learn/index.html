Bring your ID, pick your store, and let the team guide you. Here's everything worth knowing before your first trip to Good Life Collective.

Read it · 5 min

New here? Start here.

Good Life Collective runs five licensed adult-use dispensaries: Rochester, Buffalo, Canandaigua and Batavia in New York, plus Astoria, Oregon. This is where we break it all down — what to bring, how ordering works, what each category on our menus actually is, and the ground rules in each state.

Never shopped with us? Start with the first-visit guide. Wondering what's on the menu? The product guide walks through every category on the menu, from flower to accessories. Want the legal basics? Read up on New York and Oregon — general info, with links straight to the regulators. Then dig into the blog for product education and news from the Good Life Collective team.

Everything here is written for adults 21 and over, and none of it is legal or medical advice. Menus and deals change by store and by day, so check your store's menu for the latest.

21+ only. You must be 21 or older with valid government-issued ID to purchase cannabis products. Please consume responsibly.
