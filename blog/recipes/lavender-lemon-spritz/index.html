Lavender lemon spritz

A zero-proof, zero-THC sipper built on a quick homemade lavender syrup.

Recipes·2 min read·Nov 18, 2026·By Good Life Collective

Recipe card

What you need

Makes
4 drinks
Time
25 minutes, plus cooling
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 tablespoon dried culinary lavender
  • 1/2 cup fresh lemon juice (3 to 4 lemons)
  • 3 cups chilled sparkling water
  • Ice
  • Lemon wheels and lavender sprigs, to garnish

Safety first. For adults 21+ only. If anything you make is infused, label it, keep it out of reach of children and pets, and start low. Wait before having more. Never drive impaired.

Method

Step by step

  1. 01
    Step 1:

    Make the syrup: bring the water and sugar to a simmer in a small saucepan, stirring until the sugar dissolves.

  2. 02
    Step 2:

    Take the pan off the heat, stir in the lavender and let it steep for 10 minutes.

  3. 03
    Step 3:

    Strain out the lavender and let the syrup cool.

  4. 04
    Step 4:

    Fill four glasses with ice. Add 2 tablespoons of lavender syrup and 2 tablespoons of lemon juice to each.

  5. 05
    Step 5:

    Top each glass with about 3/4 cup of sparkling water, stir gently and garnish with a lemon wheel and a lavender sprig.

Tart lemon, a floral note of lavender and plenty of fizz. This spritz is alcohol-free and non-infused (no THC, nothing to measure), so it works for every table and any kind of night. It makes an easy batch for four, and the lavender syrup is the only thing you cook.

A nod to Thirsty Thursday

At Good Life Collective Rochester, Thursday is Thirsty Thursday on the weekly board: 12 Drinks For $55, 24 Drinks For $100 and Tinctures 10% OFF. That deal is for the beverage menu: cannabis drinks. This spritz has no cannabis in it. If you pick up a cannabis drink, enjoy it on its own, as labeled, and keep this one as your zero-THC sipper. Terms can change, so check the menu, or see the weekly deals guide.

Make it yours

  • Use culinary lavender, the kind sold for cooking.
  • Don't steep the lavender longer than the recipe says; it can turn bitter.
  • Sweeter? Add more syrup. Tarter? More lemon. Swap in lime for a twist.
  • Garnish counts: a lemon wheel and a lavender sprig make it look like you tried.
  • Leftover syrup keeps in a sealed jar in the fridge.
Keep it clear: this recipe is non-infused. If cannabis drinks are in the house, keep them in their original cans, labeled, and away from kids and pets. 21+ only.

Quick answers

Does this spritz contain cannabis?

No. It's non-infused and alcohol-free.

Can I make the syrup ahead?

Yes. Cool it, seal it in a jar and keep it in the fridge.
