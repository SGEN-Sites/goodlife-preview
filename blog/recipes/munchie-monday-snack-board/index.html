Recipes · Snacks

Munchie Monday, handled

Salty, sweet, crunchy and fresh on one board. No cooking, no cannabis — just the snacks.

  • Recipes
  • 2 min read
  • Nov 18, 2026
  • By Good Life Collective

Recipe card

What you need

  • MakesServes 4 to 6
  • Time20 minutes
  • 1 cup hummus or your favorite dip
  • 1 cup mixed nuts
  • 8 ounces sharp cheddar, sliced
  • 1 sleeve of crackers
  • 4 cups popcorn
  • 2 cups pretzels
  • 2 cups grapes
  • 1 pint strawberries
  • 1 cucumber and 2 carrots, cut into sticks
  • 4 ounces dark chocolate, broken into pieces
Safety first. For adults 21+ only. If anything you make is infused, label it, keep it out of reach of children and pets, and start low — wait before having more. Never drive impaired.

Method

Step by step

  1. 01
    Step 1:

    Set the dip and nuts in small bowls on a large board.

  2. 02
    Step 2:

    Fan out the cheese and crackers in loose piles.

  3. 03
    Step 3:

    Add the fruit and veg: grapes in small bunches, strawberries whole or halved, cucumber and carrot sticks.

  4. 04
    Step 4:

    Fill the gaps with popcorn and pretzels.

  5. 05
    Step 5:

    Scatter the chocolate last, then set out small plates, napkins and water.

Munchie Monday is Rochester's edibles day on the Good Life weekly board: EDIBLES BOGO 25% OFF and Buy Any Edible Get a $4 Drink. This board borrows the name and none of the cannabis — a non-infused spread of salty, sweet and fresh that comes together in about 20 minutes, no cooking required. Full deal details are in the weekly deals guide.

Build it like a pro

  • Anchor it. Bowls go down first, dip and nuts, and everything else builds around them.
  • Mix textures. Something crunchy, something creamy, something juicy.
  • Play salty against sweet. Pretzels next to chocolate, cheddar next to grapes.
  • Keep it fresh. Fruit, veg and a pitcher of water belong on every board.
  • Swap freely. Use whatever your crew actually likes.

Switch it up

  • Sweet tooth: more fruit and chocolate, fewer crackers.
  • Savory crew: add pickles, olives or sliced salami.
  • Big group: double everything and split it across two boards.
  • Make it ahead: wash and cut the produce early, then build the board right before people arrive so the crunchy stuff stays crunchy.

Want a drink on the side? Our zero-proof lavender lemon spritz pairs right up.

Keep the board non-infused

No edibles on the board. Gummies and chocolates from the dispensary look just like regular candy, so keep infused products in their original packaging, labeled, and away from kids and pets. 21+ only, and never drive impaired.

Planning to enjoy an edible alongside the snacks? Start low, go slow — here's how. More from the kitchen in our recipes.
