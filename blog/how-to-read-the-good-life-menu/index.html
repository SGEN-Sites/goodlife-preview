Read the menu like a regular

Good Life's menu cards pack a handful of key fields. Here's what each one tells you, and what it doesn't.

Every product on a Good Life menu shows up as a card. Learn to read one and you can size up anything on the menu in seconds — flower, pre-rolls, vapes, edibles, drinks and the rest — at any of our five stores.

The card, top to bottom

Choose your location, open a category, and each cannabis product card lists the brand, the product name, the strain type, the size and THC (when they're listed), then two prices: the menu price and the price with tax. Some cards also carry a deal tag. The name often holds extra detail — pack counts, infusions, the oil inside a vape — so read it all the way through.

Strain type: five labels

Cannabis products carry one of five labels: Sativa, Hybrid, Indica, Sativa-Dominant or Indica-Dominant (shortened to Sativa-Dom and Indica-Dom on the cards). It's how the product is classified, and it's a handy way to filter. What it isn't: a promise of how you'll feel. Everyone's experience is different, so use the label as a starting point and pay attention to what works for you.

THC, Total THC and CBD

On flower, pre-rolls, vapes and concentrates, potency usually shows as a percentage. The THC figure on a card is the product's Total THC when one is reported. Total THC factors in THCa — the form THC takes in raw flower, before heat converts it — along with THC itself, which makes it the fairest number to compare. CBD, when it's listed, shows on the product page. No THC number on a card? No figure was listed for that product.

Edibles and drinks work differently: they're labeled in milligrams of THC per package, like 100mg for a pack of gummies or 10mg for a can. Our edibles guide walks through the math.

Bigger isn't automatically better. A higher number means more THC on paper, not a better product for you. New, or easing back in? Sort by THC from low to high and start there.

Sizes you'll see most

Size is weight for most products and volume for drinks and tinctures. The ones that come up again and again on our menus:

  • Flower: 3.5g (an eighth) is the most common size, with 7g, 14g and 28g (an ounce) on our New York menus.
  • Pre-rolls: 1g singles lead the way, and pack names spell out the count and size — a 5pk of .7g joints is 3.5g total.
  • Vapes: carts mostly come in 0.5g and 1g; all-in-ones mostly run 0.5g to 2g.
  • Drinks: mostly 12 fl oz cans.
  • Tinctures: bottles such as 1 fl oz or 30ml.

The Size filter narrows a category to an exact weight or volume.

Two prices on every card

Each card shows the menu price with the price including tax right under it. When something's discounted, the card says so with a badge like 25% OFF. Want only the deals? Use the Deals filter to show items with a deal, or open the deals page.

Deal tags at Rochester

Rochester runs a weekly deals board, and its menu tags products with the day they're featured: Munchie Monday on edibles, Doobie Tuesday on pre-rolls, Weed Wednesday on flower and Thirsty Thursday on drinks. A tag points you to the day; the board spells out the deal. Terms can change, so check your store's menu for current terms. The whole board, day by day, is in our weekly deals guide.

Filter and sort like a regular

  • Strain Type — any of the five labels.
  • Brands — stick with the makers you like, or browse them all on the brands page.
  • Size — exact weights and volumes.
  • Deals — items with a deal.
  • Vape Type — on vapes, Disposable / AIO or Cartridge / 510. Not sure which? Read 510 carts vs all-in-one vapes.

Sorting covers Price (low to high or high to low), THC (low to high or high to low) and Name (A–Z).

Tap in for the details

Open a product and you get more: a description when the brand provides one, a potency breakdown — THC, CBD and others like THCa, CBN or CBG when they're reported — and, on many products on our New York menus, its top measured terpenes, listed from most to least. Terpenes are aroma compounds; Terpenes 101 covers what the common ones smell like.

Some products also show effect tags — Happy, Uplifted, Creative and the like. Those are the brand's own tags, not a promise; everyone's experience is different. If you like to browse that way, there's shop by feeling.

Quick answers

What's the difference between THC and Total THC?

Total THC factors in THCa — the form THC takes in raw flower, before heat converts it — along with THC. When a product reports Total THC, that is the THC figure on its card.

Which price will I pay?

Every card shows the menu price and the price with tax. Deals can change the total, so check your store's menu for current terms.

Does Indica or Sativa tell me how a product will feel?

No promises. Strain type is how a product is classified. Everyone's experience is different — start low and see how it goes.
