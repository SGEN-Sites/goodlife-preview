It's a Lifestyle. That's the Good Life tagline, and the merch is where you get to wear it. Tees, hoodies, hats, bottoms and everyday goods carry the Good Life name across our stores, and the merch shop pulls every style into one place, each one tied to the store that stocks it. Prices below are starting menu prices before tax.

Tees, from $25

The Buena Vida Tee — Spanish for good life — comes in Black, Blue or Red at Rochester, in sizes S through XXL depending on the color. Also at Rochester: the Melo Oversized Tee in Black or Dark Grey, the Good Life Goodz Shirt and the Good Life Shirt. Canandaigua stocks the GLC Lifestyle Social Club T Shirt, the GLC Buffalo T Shirt and the GLC No Bad Days Long Sleeve T Shirt, and Astoria has its own Good Life tee. Browse them all in tees.

Hoodies and crews, from $40

The Good Life Classic Hoodie comes in Black, Red or Royal at Rochester, alongside the GLC Embroidered Hoodie, the Good Life Mafia Heavyweight Hoodie, the Good Life Gameday Crewneck and the Good Life Double Zipper Hoodie. Canandaigua has the GLC Hooded Sweatshirt, and Astoria has the Good Life Hoodie. See them in hoodies.

Bottoms, too, from $30: Good Life Heavyweight Sweatpants at Rochester and — fittingly, for our Finger Lakes store — Lakestyle Swim Trunks at Canandaigua.

Hats, from $10

Start with the snapbacks: the GLC Corduroy Snapback and GLC Aqua Corduroy Snapback at Rochester, plus the GoodLife Brand Snapback Hat at Astoria. There's also a Good Life Fisherman Hat at Rochester, Teal Goodlife Corduroy Hats at Astoria, the GLC Beanie Cap at Canandaigua and the Good Life Beanie at Buffalo. All in hats.

Goods, from $1

The small stuff counts: Good Life cones and papers (Rochester and Astoria), lighters and lighter sleeves (Rochester, Buffalo, Canandaigua and Astoria), metal grinders (Buffalo and Astoria), Good Life 510 batteries (Rochester, plus a push-button one at Astoria), glass filter tips and a Good Life cup at Rochester, and stickers at Canandaigua.

Gift guide, by budget

  • $10 and under: Good Life Stickers ($1, Canandaigua), Good Life cones and papers, lighters ($3 at Astoria and Canandaigua) and the Good Life Beanie ($10, Buffalo).
  • $15 to $45: Good Life grinders and 510 batteries ($15), the Good Life Fisherman Hat ($25), the Buena Vida Tee ($25), the GLC Aqua Corduroy Snapback ($35) and the Good Life Classic Hoodie ($40).
  • Go big: the Good Life Mafia Heavyweight Hoodie ($80), plus the Good Life Double Zipper Hoodie and Good Life Heavyweight Sweatpants ($120 each).

Sizes run from XS to XXL depending on the style, so check each product page.

Style it like the lookbook

Our lookbook keeps it simple: an oversized graphic tee, a structured snapback and light-wash denim, shorts or straight-leg. Grab a tee and a hat and you're most of the way there.

Winning the War on Drugs

Look closely at the tees in our lookbook shoot and you'll find it: Winning the War on Drugs — a globe ringed by the words War on Drugs, signed It's a Lifestyle. The same design lives on the Good Life enamel pin, where the Good Life script sits right on the globe.

Where to find it

Merch lives on each store's menu, so the lineup depends on the store. Rochester carries the widest range; Canandaigua, Astoria and Buffalo stock their own pieces. Prices here were current when we wrote this, so check the menu before you buy. Like everything on this site, it's for adults 21+.

At Rochester, the weekly board takes MERCH 10% OFF ALL DAY on Doobie Tuesday and again on Sunday Bun(dle) Day — see the weekly deals guide. Shopping for someone else? When we wrote this, Canandaigua's menu had gift card bundles that pair a gift card with a Good Life shirt or sweatshirt.

Quick answers

Can I order merch online?

Merch is on each store's menu, so you can add it to an online order for in-store pickup at the store that stocks it — or delivery where available.

Which store has the most merch?

Rochester carries the widest range. Canandaigua, Astoria and Buffalo stock their own pieces.

Is merch ever on sale?

At Rochester, the weekly board lists MERCH 10% OFF ALL DAY on Doobie Tuesday and Sunday Bun(dle) Day. Terms can change, so check the menu.
