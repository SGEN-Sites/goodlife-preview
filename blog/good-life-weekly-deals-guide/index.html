Thursday Thirsty Thursday

Drinks day, with tinctures in on it too. Most drinks on our menus are 12 fl oz cans labeled in mg of THC per can. Want a zero-THC sipper on the side? Make our lavender lemon spritz.

Friday TGIF

TGIF stands for Thank GoodLife Its Friday, and the board splits the day into two windows: 9am–3pm and 4:20pm–7:10pm, each with its own deal. Rochester is open 9am–10pm on Fridays.

Plan your week

  • Edibles? Monday.
  • Pre-roll packs or merch? Tuesday, and merch again on Sunday.
  • Flower? Wednesday.
  • Drinks or tinctures? Thursday.
  • A bit of everything? Friday, 4:20pm–7:10pm, when the board says 15% OFF STOREWIDE.
  • Vapes or concentrates? Saturday.
  • Big basket? Sunday's deal pays you back on your next order.

Make the most of it

  • Order ahead online for in-store pickup at Rochester, or get delivery: fast, free delivery right to your door. *Free delivery requires a minimum order of $50.
  • Look for the tags. Rochester's menu tags products for Munchie Monday, Doobie Tuesday, Weed Wednesday and Thirsty Thursday, so you can spot them while you browse.
  • Join Good Life Rewards. Earn points on every purchase and unlock exclusive discounts — details on the loyalty page.
  • Get the app to earn points, get exclusive deals and stay updated with dispensary notifications. Download it here.
  • Bring ID. You must be 21 or older with valid government-issued ID to purchase cannabis products.

Running at our other stores

Rochester's board isn't the whole story. When we wrote this, Buffalo had 25% off STIIIZY vapes, and Canandaigua was running Hometown Heroes: Buy (3) of any of our Canandaigua pre-rolls for $18 total (save $6)! Rochester also had STIIIZY 25% OFF and the 6 Point Last Chance Deal: Buy any 6 point product, get 10% off. Deals rotate; check the deals page and your store's menu for current terms.

Quick answers

Is the weekly board at every Good Life store?

No — it's Rochester's board. Some of our other stores run their own deals; check the deals page and your store's menu.

Can deal terms change?

Yes. Terms can change, so check your store's menu for current terms before you order.

What are Rochester's hours?

Mon–Thu 9am–9pm, Fri–Sat 9am–10pm, Sun 10am–9pm.
