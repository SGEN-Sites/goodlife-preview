Good Life is family-owned and community-minded, and that shows up off the sales floor too. This year's calendar had a career fair in Buffalo, a week of 420 deals and a PUFF pop-up in Canandaigua, and a carnival in Rochester. Here's the recap, event by event.

The 2026 lineup

Buffalo Industry Career Fair

Tuesday, March 31, 2026 · 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM · Good Life Collective, 577 Forest Avenue, Buffalo. Good Life Collective hosted this one at its Buffalo store, set up to bring together employers, industry professionals and job seekers from across Western New York, with career opportunities in retail, cannabis, food service and other local industries.

It was pitched at anyone exploring a new career path, looking to network with professionals or searching for a next job, with the chance to meet employers, learn about career paths in emerging industries and get guidance on entering and growing within the workforce.

What was on the agenda:

  • Direct access to hiring companies
  • Networking opportunities with local businesses
  • Career paths to explore across multiple industries
  • Guidance and feedback from industry professionals

Wil's BBQ Food Truck was set to be on site serving fresh food, and the fair was 21+ to attend. Good Life Collective hosted it as a licensed cannabis dispensary "committed to breaking stigma while creating opportunities for workforce development and community engagement in Buffalo."

420 Deal Days

From Monday, April 13, 2026 · Good Life Collective: Finger Lakes (Canandaigua). The promise: new deals every day of the week leading up to 4/20, big savings on flower, vapes and pre-rolls, and giveaways on 4/20 itself, with all deals running through 4/20 or until product sold out. Finger Lakes is the name on our Canandaigua store's own banners, so that's the store that ran it.

PUFF Pop-Up

Friday, April 24, 2026 · Good Life Collective: Canandaigua. The invitation kept it short: "Join the fun as we have PUFF in the house!" PUFF is one of the brands on our New York menus, with pre-rolls, flower and vapes on the shelves. A brand in the house is a good excuse to ask questions and learn what they make.

The Good Life Carnival

Sunday, September 6, 2026 · 3PM–10PM · Rochester Beer Park, 375 Averill Avenue, Rochester. The Good Life Collective Carnival was billed this way: "Bringing together Rochester's cannabis lifestyle community, local businesses, and national brands for an unforgettable day of entertainment, food, games, music, and culture." The poster called it an end-of-summer celebration and promised food, merch, games, vendors, giveaways and live entertainment — 21+, with valid ID required.

Why we show up

Family-owned. Community-minded. Professionally curated. That's how Good Life describes itself, and the calendar backs it up: jobs and networking in Buffalo, deals and a brand pop-up in the Finger Lakes, and a full day of food, games and live entertainment in Rochester.

What's next

All four of these have wrapped. To stay in the loop with our latest happenings, keep an eye on the events page, follow @glc_585 (opens in a new tab) on Instagram, and get the Good Life app for dispensary notifications. Questions about an event? Use our contact page, and the team will get back to you soon.

Between events, find us at our five stores: Rochester at 155 Monroe Ave, Buffalo at 577 Forest Ave, Canandaigua at 230 S. Main St, Batavia at 230 West Main St. and Astoria, Oregon at 193 Marine Dr.

Hearing about us for the first time? Good Life Collective is owned and operated by the Hernandez family, who also run our store in Astoria, Oregon. Get the full story on our about page.

Quick answers

Are Good Life events 21+?

The career fair and the carnival were both 21+. Check each event's details, and bring valid government-issued ID.

Where can I find upcoming events?

On the events page, through the Good Life app's dispensary notifications, and on Instagram at @glc_585.
