Pre-rolls, decoded

Singles, packs, dogwalkers, infused and blunts: how to tell them apart on our menus.

A pre-roll is exactly what it sounds like: ground flower, rolled for you and ready to light. No grinder, no papers, no rolling skills required. Pre-rolls are one of the deepest sections on every Good Life menu, and the formats go way past the classic single. Here's how to tell them apart.

Singles

The classic. Most singles on our menus are 1g, with half-grams and other sizes in the mix. A single is the easy way to try a strain before you commit to a whole eighth. You'll also see bigger singles, like 1.5g and 2g, which are easy to share.

Multi-packs

Packs bundle several smaller joints, and the name usually does the math for you: a 5pk of .7g joints is 3.5g total, and so is a 7pk of .5g. Across our menus you'll find 2-, 3-, 5-, 7- and 10-packs: most single-strain, some mixed.

Packs make sense when you're sharing, or when you want the same strain across a few sessions. Close the tube or pack between uses so the rest don't dry out.

Small joints sold in packs often go by dogwalkers: short, quick and easy to keep on hand. Check the name for the size of each one.

Infused pre-rolls

Infused pre-rolls add concentrate to the flower, and the names tell you what's in there: diamond infused, live resin infused, live rosin infused, hash infused or kief coated. When we checked, infused pre-rolls on our menus listed noticeably higher THC numbers than plain ones, so read the card before you spark, and start low.

Infused or not, check the size of each joint before you light one solo. A 1.5g infused pre-roll is a lot of pre-roll.

Blunts

A blunt is rolled in a wrap instead of a paper. Depending on the store, our menus carry 1g blunts, infused blunts (kief-infused, for one), moon rock blunts and infused mini blunts. A blunt is a classic pass-around, so check the size and the THC on the card before you light up.

Lighting up, done right

  • Toast the tip evenly, turning it as you go, before your first pull.
  • Pull gently. Hard, fast pulls make it burn hot and uneven.
  • If it starts to run down one side, turn it so the slower side burns down.
  • Let it rest between puffs, and put it out properly when you're done.

Smoke smart

  • 21+ only. Take a puff or two, then wait and see how you feel before more.
  • Enjoy it in private, out of public view.
  • Keep pre-rolls, lighters and papers away from kids and pets.
  • Never drive impaired.

Rolling your own instead? Good Life cones, papers and lighters are in the merch shop, each tied to the store that stocks it. Keeping a few for later? Store them sealed and away from heat. See how to store cannabis.

Quick answers

What's the difference between a pre-roll and a blunt?

Most pre-rolls are rolled in paper; a blunt is rolled in a wrap. Both come ready to light.

Are infused pre-rolls stronger?

They add concentrate to the flower, and on our menus they typically list higher THC numbers than plain pre-rolls. Read the card and start low.

What's a dogwalker?

A small joint, usually sold in packs. The product name tells you the size of each one.
