510 cart or all-in-one?

Cartridge / 510 or Disposable / AIO? How the two vape types on our menus differ, where batteries come in, and how to pick.

Open the vapes menu at any Good Life store and the Vape Type filter gives you two choices: Cartridge / 510 and Disposable / AIO. Both hold cannabis oil, both are inhaled, and both are all over our shelves. The difference is the hardware, and whether you need to bring your own battery.

Cartridge / 510

A 510 cart is a small tank of oil with a threaded base that screws onto a separate battery. "510" is the name of that thread (a widely used standard), so most 510 carts pair with most 510 batteries. Buy the battery once, then swap carts whenever you want something new.

On our menus, carts mostly come in 0.5g and 1g. The names tell you what's inside, as the brand describes it: live resin, live rosin, liquid diamonds or distillate. A few are sold as rechargeable starter kits, and the product name will say so.

Batteries are sold separately

A cart can't power itself. No battery at home? Add one to your order. You'll find 510 batteries in Accessories on most of our menus, and there's Good Life-branded hardware too: a Good Life 510 battery at Rochester and Buffalo, and a Goodlife push-button 510 battery at Astoria. Canandaigua's accessories include .510 batteries from other makers.

One curveball: pods. If the name says Pod (our menus carry STIIIZY and PAX pods), it fits that brand's own battery, not a standard 510. Match the pod to the battery made for it. The Good Life 510 batteries from Rochester and Astoria are also listed in our merch shop, tied to the store that stocks them.

Disposable / AIO

An all-in-one is exactly what it sounds like: the battery and the oil come built into one device. Nothing to screw on, nothing extra to buy, and when the oil runs out, the device is done, which is why the menu files them with disposables.

All-in-ones are the bigger group on our vape menus, mostly running 0.5g to 2g. Look for AIO, All in One, Disposable or Palm in the name. Like carts, they come in live resin, live rosin, liquid diamond and distillate versions.

First time with either

  • Cart: charge the battery first, then screw the cart on finger-tight. No need to crank it.
  • All-in-one: check the package to see whether it has a button or fires when you draw.
  • Either way: one small puff, then wait. Inhaled cannabis shows up faster than edibles, but everyone's experience is different.
  • Not sure it fits? Ask at the counter before you buy. The Good Life team is here to guide you.

Rule of thumb: already have a battery and like variety? Carts are your lane. Want to grab it and go? All-in-one.

Vape smart

  • 21+ only, and buy from licensed dispensaries only, like every Good Life store.
  • Start low, go slow. Give each puff a few minutes before the next.
  • Keep carts and devices out of hot cars and direct sun, and away from kids and pets.
  • Never drive impaired.

More on keeping your stash in shape: how to store cannabis. For every category we carry, see the product guide.

Quick answers

Do I need a battery for an all-in-one?

No. All-in-ones and disposables have the battery built in. 510 cartridges need a separate battery, and pods need their own brand's battery.

Will any 510 battery work with any 510 cart?

510 is a widely used standard thread, so most carts and batteries pair up. Pods are different. They fit their own brand's battery.

Where do I find a battery on the menu?

In Accessories on most of our menus. Rochester and Buffalo carry a Good Life 510 battery, and Astoria carries a Goodlife push-button 510 battery.
