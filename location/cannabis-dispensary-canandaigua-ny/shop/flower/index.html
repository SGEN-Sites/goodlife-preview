Can I order flower online at Good Life Canandaigua?

Yes. Browse and order online for in-store pickup or delivery where available. Hit “Shop Now” to get started.

Do I need to be 21 to shop?

Yes. You must be 21 or older with valid government-issued ID to purchase cannabis products. Please consume responsibly.

Does Good Life Canandaigua deliver?

Good Life Canandaigua is pickup only: order online, then collect your order in store at 230 S. Main St, Canandaigua, NY 14424. Delivery is available from the Rochester and Buffalo stores.
