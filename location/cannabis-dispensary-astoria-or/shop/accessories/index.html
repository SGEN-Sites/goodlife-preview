Good to know

Quick answers before you order accessories from Good Life Astoria.

Full Astoria menuStore info

Can I order accessories online at Good Life Astoria?

Yes — browse and order online for in-store pickup or delivery where available. Hit “Shop Now” to get started.

Do I need to be 21 to shop?

Yes. You must be 21 or older with valid government-issued ID to purchase cannabis products. Please consume responsibly.

Does Good Life Astoria deliver?

Good Life Astoria is pickup only: order online, then collect your order in store at 193 Marine Dr, Astoria, OR 97103. Delivery is available from the Rochester and Buffalo stores.
