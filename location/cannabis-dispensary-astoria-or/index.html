Frequently asked questions

All FAQs
What are your store hours?

Hours vary by location. Rochester is open Mon–Thu 9am–9pm, Fri–Sat 9am–10pm, Sun 10am–9pm. Buffalo is Mon–Thu 9am–9pm, Fri–Sat 9am–11pm, Sun 10am–8pm. Check our footer or contact us for other locations.

Do you offer online ordering?

Yes. Browse and order online for in-store pickup or delivery where available. Hit “Shop Now” to get started.

Is Good Life Collective family owned?

Yes. Good Life Collective is owned and operated by the Hernandez family, who also run a location in Astoria, Oregon. Family-owned, community-focused.

Where are your locations?

We have dispensaries in Rochester, Buffalo, Canandaigua, and Batavia, NY, plus Astoria, Oregon. Select a location from our home page to shop your nearest store.

Do I need to be 21 to shop?

Yes. You must be 21 or older with valid government-issued ID to purchase cannabis products. Please consume responsibly.

How can I contact you?

Call your nearest location directly, use the contact info in our footer, or visit us in store. Our team is always happy to help.
