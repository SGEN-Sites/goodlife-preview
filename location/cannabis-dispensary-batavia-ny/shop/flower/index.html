Can I order flower online at Good Life Batavia?

Yes — browse and order online for in-store pickup or delivery where available. Hit “Shop Now” to get started.

Do I need to be 21 to shop?

Yes. You must be 21 or older with valid government-issued ID to purchase cannabis products. Please consume responsibly.

Does Good Life Batavia deliver?

Good Life Batavia is pickup only: order online, then collect your order in store at 230 West Main St., Batavia, NY 14020. Delivery is available from the Rochester and Buffalo stores.
