About this product from Rec Roots

Jezus Juice is a premium indica flower, appreciated for its vibrant aroma, smooth character, and expressive terpene profile. Jezus Juice is known for its balanced and flavorful profile, offering a harmonious blend of uplifting brightness and gentle body ease. Its overall character feels versatile and approachable, making it suitable for relaxed daytime sessions or easygoing evenings. Its terpene-rich expression highlights sweet fruit notes layered with subtle herbal undertones and a light earthy finish, creating a smooth and aromatic experience that appeals to fans of modern, fruit-forward cultivars.
