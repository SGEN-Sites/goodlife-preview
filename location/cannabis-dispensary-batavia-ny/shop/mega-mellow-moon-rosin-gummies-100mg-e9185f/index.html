About this product from Gron

GRON's Mellow Moon Mega gummy delivers 100 mg of indica-powered relaxation in a deliciously unique yuzu melon flavor. Crafted with premium hash rosin, this edible offers a dreamy, full-bodied experience perfect for unwinding after a long day. The bright citrus notes of yuzu blend seamlessly with sweet melon, creating a refreshing taste that makes your relaxation ritual something to look forward to. Each Mellow Moon Mega is designed for those seeking deep relaxation and tranquil calm. GRON's commitment to quality shines through in every bite, combining exceptional flavor with the pure, concentrated power of hash rosin. Treat yourself to a dreamy escape with Mellow Moon Mega—where premium cannabis meets irresistible taste.
